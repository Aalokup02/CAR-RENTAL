Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about driver licenses, cancellations, security deposits, and insurance coverages.

What do I need to rent a car?

You will need a valid driver's license, an ID proof (like a passport, Aadhar card, or Voter ID), and a credit/debit card under your name to hold the refundable security deposit during your rental duration.

Is there a daily mileage limit?

Most of our hybrid and standard fuel cars include unlimited mileage. For fully electric vehicles (like Tesla S/Y), we provide a generous 400km range per day, with a small per-kilometer fee applied to extra mileage.

Can I cancel or modify my booking?

Yes, DriveElite offers free cancellation and modifications up to 24 hours prior to your scheduled pickup time. Cancellations inside the 24-hour window may incur a small late processing fee equivalent to a one-day rental.

Are there any hidden costs?

Absolutely not. We pride ourselves on transparent billing. The final price calculated in your booking summary includes mandatory insurances, local taxes, and vat. Optional add-ons (like child seats or personal drivers) are shown explicitly.
